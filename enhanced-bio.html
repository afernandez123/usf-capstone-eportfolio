Professional Biography

Antonio Fernandez is a student at the University of South Florida pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Information Science with a concentration in Information Security. His academic interests focus on cybersecurity, information systems, project management, and the role of technology in protecting organizations and users.

Through his coursework, Antonio has developed a stronger understanding of how information is organized, managed, protected, and used within professional environments. His studies have helped him build knowledge in areas such as database management, cybersecurity principles, software systems, and information security practices.

Antonio is interested in pursuing a career in cybersecurity, with a focus on roles such as SOC analyst, cybersecurity analyst, or cloud security analyst. He is especially interested in learning how organizations monitor threats, protect data, manage security tools, and respond to cyber incidents.

His strengths include problem solving, adaptability, technical curiosity, and a willingness to continue learning. As technology continues to change, Antonio understands the importance of staying current with new tools, security practices, and industry expectations.

This ePortfolio highlights Antonio's academic work, professional development, and growth throughout the capstone course. It includes selected assignments, reflections, and projects that demonstrate his progress as an Information Science student and his preparation for future work in the information security field.
