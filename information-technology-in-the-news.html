Capstone Assignment

Information Policy/Technology in the News

Student Learning Outcome #2: Appraise current information technology events or legislative actions in the news.

Download PDF Download Word Document

Assignment Details

Policy topic
Artificial intelligence legislation in the United States
News source
IEEE InSight
Legislation traced
AI Incident Reporting Act
Evidence format
APA-formatted essay with references

This essay examines the policy challenge created by stalled federal artificial intelligence legislation and the resulting differences among state-level approaches. It identifies the core issue as the absence of a cohesive national framework for AI governance while the technology is increasingly used in employment, financial services, education, healthcare, and public communication.

The paper connects this current news event to the legislative tracing exercise by discussing the proposed AI Incident Reporting Act. That bill would require developers of advanced AI systems to report dangerous capabilities, security vulnerabilities, and significant safety issues. The essay evaluates how focused requirements for reporting, transparency, human oversight, data documentation, and algorithmic auditing could help establish public accountability while preserving responsible innovation.

Stakeholders considered include federal and state legislators, technology companies, consumers, educational institutions, researchers, information professionals, and society more broadly. The assessment concludes that information professionals will need to understand both the technical and regulatory dimensions of AI as they help organizations manage data, evaluate systems, and protect privacy and security.

Reflection: This artifact demonstrates my ability to connect a current technology-policy event with legislative research, identify affected stakeholders, and assess implications for information professionals and society.
